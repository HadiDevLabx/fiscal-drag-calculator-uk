# Fiscal Drag Calculator UK — What the Threshold Freeze Costs You

> Prices are up 29.8% since April 2021 while the £12,570 personal allowance has not moved once.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Fiscal drag is a tax rise that is never announced. Thresholds stay still, wages rise with inflation, and more of every pay packet falls into higher bands.

The personal allowance has been frozen at £12,570 since April 2021 and is frozen until 2028. Had it tracked CPI it would now be about £16,315.

That difference, roughly £3,745, is income that used to be tax-free and now is not.

The same freeze applies to the £50,270 higher-rate threshold, which is why the number of higher-rate taxpayers has grown so fast without any rate changing.

## What this does not cover

Source for the price index is ONS CPI series D7BT, July 2026. Your own experience of inflation may differ from the national basket.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
